# ParkPlus
ParkPlus - A digital amusement park companion

ParkPlus helps visitors get more out of a day at the park: browse attractions, manage tickets, and move through the park with less hassle, all from their phone or browser.

**Features:**
- Authentication: sign up, log in, and role-based access (visitor / admin)
- Ticketing: purchase and generate digital tickets with a unique code or QR
- Gate validation: staff can scan or validate tickets at entry points
- Attractions: browse rides and attractions with details such as height limits and status
- Wait times: see live queue information 
- Park map: find rides, food, and facilities 
- Admin dashboard: manage attractions, tickets, and users

**Tech Stack:**
- Frontend: React
- Backend: Node.js, Express
- Database: MongoDB (Mongoose)
- Auth:	JWT
- Hosting:	Vercel
